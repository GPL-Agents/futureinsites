Design Philosophy

How Greg approaches building AI systems.

Five principles applied to every AI platform, whether starting from zero or inheriting something already in production.

01
Human-in-the-Loop Validation

Tiered autonomy, feedback capture as ground truth, and trust calibration over time. Not just a human approval button.

02
Model Fallback and Cost-Quality Optimization

Task-based model routing, fallback chains from LLM to rules to human escalation, and token economics baked in from day one.

03
Data Governance, Lineage and Privacy

Data classification, RAG provenance, PII handling, and SOC 2 / GDPR compliance built at ingestion, not retrofitted after launch.

04
Responsible AI and Guardrails

Red-teaming, input/output filtering, bias audits, and risk tiers before anything ships. Production requirements, not policy theater.

05
Observability, Evals and Continuous Learning

Full inference telemetry, automated evals on every model or prompt change, drift detection, and a fine-tuning loop on production feedback.
