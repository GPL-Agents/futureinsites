Design Philosophy

How I approach building AI systems.

Five principles I apply to every AI platform I design — whether starting from zero or inheriting something in production.

01
Human-in-the-Loop Validation
  • Tiered autonomy by task risk & reversibility
  • Feedback capture as ground truth for evals and fine-tuning
  • Agentic checkpoints at high-stakes decision nodes
  • Trust calibration over time via override rate tracking
02
Model Fallback & Cost-Quality Optimization
  • Multi-model routing by complexity, latency, cost
  • Fallback chains: LLM → rules → human escalation
  • Token economics: caching, compression, batching
  • Model registry with benchmark governance
03
Data Governance, Lineage & Privacy
  • Data classification taxonomy at ingestion
  • RAG grounding with provenance and freshness SLAs
  • PII redaction, right-to-be-forgotten in vector stores
  • SOC 2 / GDPR / CCPA compliance alignment
04
Responsible AI & Guardrails
  • Pre-deployment red-teaming and adversarial testing
  • Input/output filtering and content safety layer
  • Bias audits and fairness analysis before launch
  • Risk tiers, model cards, escalation playbook
05
Observability, Evals & Continuous Learning
  • Full inference telemetry: prompt, response, cost, feedback
  • Automated eval suite on every model or prompt change
  • Drift detection, A/B gating, canary deployments
  • RLHF / DPO fine-tuning loop on production feedback
