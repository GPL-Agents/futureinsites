How We Work

Our Approach

FutureInSites helps small and mid-sized businesses move beyond AI experimentation. We identify high-impact opportunities, implement practical solutions, and transfer ownership so your team can run it confidently.

Approach

Build. Customize. Train.

Most companies are already experimenting with AI: working groups, lunch-and-learns, pilot projects, and teams sharing the latest thing they tried in ChatGPT. Exploring AI is easy. Embedding it into everyday work is where real value is created.

Stage 1

Build

Map your AI opportunities, build the business case, and define the roadmap. Grounded in your priorities, your workflows, and your data.

You leave with a clear picture of where AI can help and a specific first project to pursue.

  • AI Opportunity Map
  • Use Case Prioritization
  • Business Case & Roadmap
→
Stage 2

Customize

Tailor tools, workflows, and governance to your specific operations. Vendor selection, agent configuration, and process redesign built for how you work.

We fit the solution to the tools, processes, and people already in your business.

  • Workflow Redesign
  • Vendor & Tool Selection
  • Governance Guardrails
→
Stage 3

Train

Enable your team with practical tools, playbooks, and hands-on training. We transfer ownership so the capability sticks.

Your team can use and maintain it confidently without depending on us.

  • Team Enablement
  • Playbooks & Prompts
  • 30/60/90-Day Roadmap
For teams that want ongoing support after Train, Embedded AI Engineering is available — new agents, expanded workflows, tool integrations, and skills development as your needs evolve. Think of it as a part-time AI team that keeps your stack current: adding a scheduling agent, wiring in a new CRM, building out an intake or quoting workflow, or training staff on new capabilities as they come online. For teams with the capacity to run it themselves, we hand off cleanly with documentation, playbooks, and a clear owner for every system we built together.
Signature Engagement

AI Transformation Session

A 2-day onsite intensive that takes your leadership team from "we should do something with AI" to a prioritized roadmap and live development. We come onsite with pre-researched analysis of your business; you leave with both clarity and momentum.

Inquire about scheduling →

The AI Landscape

Chart your path through the landscape. Readiness assessment, prioritized AI application analysis, buy-vs-build evaluation, and a sequenced roadmap built on pre-researched analysis of your business.
