How we work with you

FutureInSites works with small and medium businesses, professional offices, and individuals who want AI working for them without needing a technical team to make it happen.

Small & Medium Businesses

AI strategy and implementation for field-first businesses

For construction companies, MEP contractors, industrial services firms, field trades, and suppliers: businesses where worker time is the most valuable asset and a dedicated IT team is not in the budget. We bring enterprise methodology at SMB scale, with engagements that follow a Build, Customize, Train model: we build the foundation, tailor it to your operations, and train your team to own it.

AI Build & Integration

Build and integrate AI capability across your business. Custom agent development for specific workflows, multi-step workflow orchestration across internal and external systems, and third-party AI platform integration (OpenAI, Anthropic, vertical SaaS).

Built for organizations that need ongoing engineering capacity without standing up an internal AI team.

ABOUT

Our Story

We connect technology to real business problems, building practical solutions that improve operations, support growth, and create measurable value.

The beginning

I have always been a builder

In high school and college, I framed houses. There was something deeply satisfying about starting with an architect's design and gradually watching it become real. A foundation became a frame. A drawing became rooms, walls, and a roof. Eventually, it became a place where people would live.

I loved the physical work, but what stayed with me was the experience of bringing an idea to life and knowing that what we built would be useful to someone.

I still build today. The materials have changed.

Instead of lumber and plans, I work with technology, data, products, business processes, and people. I help architect systems, assemble the right capabilities, and guide the work from an initial idea to something operating in the real world.

The satisfaction is still the same. It comes from seeing a design become functional, watching people begin to use it, and knowing that something we built has made a meaningful difference.

That instinct has shaped my entire career and is the reason I founded FutureInSites.

Our belief

Helping businesses grow through better use of technology

FutureInSites is built on a simple belief: technology is valuable when it solves a real business problem.

AI is creating powerful new possibilities, but it is not the first major technology shift businesses have had to navigate, and it will not be the last. The challenge is rarely gaining access to technology. The challenge is understanding where it can create value, choosing the right approach, and making it work inside a real organization.

AI may be the answer, part of the answer, or simply one useful tool among several.

The objective is not to use the most advanced technology available. It is to build something that helps the business work better.

Background

A business builder first

Where it started: performance marketing and first business

I began my career in performance marketing, where technology, data, customer behavior, and business results were inseparable. Success depended on understanding what motivated people, measuring what worked, and continually improving the systems behind growth.

I later founded FreeScholarshipGuide and built it from the ground up. I managed the operations, team, marketing, product direction, and financial decisions required to turn an idea into a profitable business generating $4 million in annual revenue. The company was acquired, but the more lasting lesson was learning what technology decisions feel like from an owner's perspective. Every investment competes for limited time and capital. Every new system has to earn its place.

Leading at scale: Nelnet

I carried that perspective into larger organizations. At Nelnet, I ran a $35 million marketing services business and led an organization of more than 50 people. The work required more than technology or marketing expertise. It required financial accountability, team development, operational discipline, and a clear understanding of how each decision affected the broader business.

Building through periods of change

Much of my career has taken place at moments when technology was changing how businesses operated and competed. At ownerIQ and Inmar Intelligence, I led products across identity resolution, audience targeting, attribution, retail media, commerce data, and real-time advertising. The work included systems operating across more than 20 advertising exchanges, an identity graph covering more than 200 million U.S. consumers, and technology connecting digital activity with offline purchasing behavior.

At MotiveMetrics, I led product and marketing for an AI-driven platform supporting more than $100 million in monthly paid-search spending. I worked across machine learning, natural-language processing, retrieval-augmented generation, vector embeddings, LLM workflows, agentic systems, platform integrations, governance, security, and reliability. I also served as acting general manager of the managed services business, where I was accountable for financial performance as well as technology and client outcomes.

Across FreeScholarshipGuide, Innovation Ads, and ownerIQ, I helped build the products, teams, and technology platforms behind three successful company exits.

The consistent pattern

Through each stage, the thread has been stepping into periods of change, learning quickly, and building the products, teams, and operating structures required for the next phase of growth.

Why we exist

Why FutureInSites

The technologies have changed throughout my career, but the underlying challenge has remained remarkably consistent. Businesses do not struggle because they lack access to tools. They struggle because someone has to connect those tools to the business.

That requires understanding the economics of the problem, the workflow surrounding it, the people affected by it, the systems it must connect with, and the changes required to make it genuinely useful.

FutureInSites brings those pieces together.

We help clients identify worthwhile opportunities, choose the right tools, build practical solutions, and introduce them in ways their teams can understand and sustain. Sometimes AI is the product being built. Sometimes it improves an existing process or system. Sometimes the right recommendation is to address a more fundamental business or technology issue first.

We start with the problem, not the tool.

Our approach

How I work

I begin by learning how the business actually operates. What takes too long? Where does information get lost? What frustrates customers or employees? Which processes depend too heavily on one person? Where could better data, automation, or decision support make a meaningful difference?

From there, I help separate interesting possibilities from useful ones. The right first project should be valuable enough to matter, focused enough to complete, and practical enough for the organization to adopt. It should create confidence and capability, not dependency.

Clients work directly with me throughout the process. I stay involved from the initial business diagnosis through solution design, implementation, training, and improvement.

How we operate

Principles behind the work

These are the operating beliefs that guide every engagement and every recommendation we make.

Start with the problem

Technology choices come after we understand the business outcome. The tool follows the need, not the other way around.

Make value visible

A project should have a clear reason to exist and a practical way to determine whether it is working. No vague success criteria.

Design for the people using it

The best system is not the one with the most features. It is the one people can understand, trust, and use effectively.

Use AI deliberately

AI is powerful, but it is not automatically the right solution. We use it when its capabilities genuinely match the problem.

Build capability, not dependency

Clients should understand what has been built and retain control over their tools, processes, data, and knowledge.

Build for change

A useful system should solve today's problem without creating a dead end. We design with flexibility to adapt as business and technology evolve.
