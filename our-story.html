ABOUT

Our Story

We help small and midsize businesses use AI to save time, make better decisions, and take repetitive work off their teams' plates.

Why clients call us

Practical questions, practical answers

Most business owners don't need another presentation on AI's potential. They need answers to questions like these:

We help answer those questions, then help put the answers into practice.

  • Where could AI make a real difference in our business?
  • Which ideas are worth pursuing, and which are distractions?
  • How can our people use AI productively and safely?
  • Can we automate a repetitive process without disrupting the business?
  • Are we getting the most from the AI features in tools we already pay for?

What we do

Where we help

  • Team training: prompt writing, AI best practices, and habits that make everyday tools more useful.
  • Governance: clear policies on how your team uses AI, what data it can touch, and who reviews the output.
  • Business LLM setup: business versions of tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Microsoft Copilot, configured for your data and your people.
  • Agents and agentic workflows: automating research, reporting, customer follow-up, and other multi-step work, with people reviewing what matters.
  • Agents in software you already use: setting up or tuning the AI built into your CRM, help desk, or other platforms.

Our approach

How we work

We start by learning how the business actually runs: what takes too long, where information gets lost, and which processes depend too heavily on one person. Then we pick a focused first project with a clear benefit and a realistic path to adoption.

We don't build dependency. You'll understand what's been built and keep control of your tools, data, processes, and knowledge.

Who you'll work with
