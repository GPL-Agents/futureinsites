ABOUT

Our Story

We connect technology to real business problems, building practical solutions that improve operations, support growth, and create measurable value.

The beginning

I have always been a builder

In high school and college, I framed houses. There was something deeply satisfying about starting with an architect's design and gradually watching it become real. A foundation became a frame. A drawing became rooms, walls, and a roof. Eventually, it became a place where people would live.

I loved the physical work, but what stayed with me was the experience of bringing an idea to life and knowing that what we built would be useful to someone.

I still build today. The materials have changed.

Instead of lumber and plans, I work with technology, data, products, business processes, and people. I help architect systems, assemble the right capabilities, and guide the work from an initial idea to something operating in the real world.

The satisfaction is still the same. It comes from seeing a design become functional, watching people begin to use it, and knowing that something we built has made a meaningful difference.

That instinct has shaped my entire career and is the reason I founded FutureInSites.

Our belief

Helping businesses grow through better use of technology

FutureInSites is built on a simple belief: technology is valuable when it solves a real business problem.

AI is creating powerful new possibilities, but it is not the first major technology shift businesses have had to navigate, and it will not be the last. The challenge is rarely gaining access to technology. The challenge is understanding where it can create value, choosing the right approach, and making it work inside a real organization.

AI may be the answer, part of the answer, or simply one useful tool among several.

The objective is not to use the most advanced technology available. It is to build something that helps the business work better.

Background

A business builder first

You work directly with the people who build it

When you work with FutureInSites, you work directly with the team that owns the outcome. Greg leads every engagement and stays involved from business diagnosis through solution design, implementation, and improvement. Depending on the scope of work, an AI strategist or a forward deployed engineer from the team may join -- experienced people we have worked with before, not contractors assigned to fill a seat. No sales team handoffs, no account manager delegation, no junior bench. The people who understand your problem are the people building the solution.

Experience on both sides of the table

FutureInSites was founded by someone who has been a founder, owner, and operator -- and the team around it brings that same orientation. We have built companies from nothing into profitable, acquired businesses. We have run large organizations with full profit-and-loss accountability. We know what it feels like to make technology investment decisions when the money comes out of your own budget, your margins are tight, and your team is counting on you to get it right.

That perspective is rare in AI consulting, where most engagements are led by people who have only worked inside large organizations or only worked in agencies. Neither background prepares you to understand the risk, accountability, and practical constraints an owner or executive faces. We have been accountable on both sides, and that shapes every recommendation we make.

Built at every scale

We have built technology and teams at small companies, mid-market organizations, and large enterprises. We have led systems handling hundreds of millions of dollars in revenue, platforms reaching more than 200 million consumers, and AI systems supporting more than $100 million in monthly ad spend. We have also run businesses with fewer than five people, where every decision was amplified because there was no room for waste.

That range matters because the right answer depends on context. We can help a five-person legal practice determine whether an AI workflow makes financial sense. We can help a mid-market manufacturer evaluate whether to build or buy an agent system. We can help an enterprise team structure AI governance across a complex organization. The questions differ, but the approach does not: understand the economics, understand the workflow, understand what changes people can actually absorb, then build something practical.

We understand what keeps you up at night

We have been the people asking: Is this investment going to pay off? How do we know we are choosing the right technology? What happens if it does not work? How do we ensure our team can actually use this? Those questions are not theoretical to us. They are the questions we have asked in our own businesses and helped clients navigate for years.

The difference between a project that works and one that stalls is rarely the technology. It is whether the team leading the work genuinely understands the operating reality of the organization they are helping. That is the perspective FutureInSites brings to every engagement.

Why we exist

Why FutureInSites

The technologies have changed throughout our careers, but the underlying challenge has remained remarkably consistent. Businesses do not struggle because they lack access to tools. They struggle because someone has to connect those tools to the business.

That requires understanding the economics of the problem, the workflow surrounding it, the people affected by it, the systems it must connect with, and the changes required to make it genuinely useful.

FutureInSites brings those pieces together.

We help clients identify worthwhile opportunities, choose the right tools, build practical solutions, and introduce them in ways their teams can understand and sustain. Sometimes AI is the product being built. Sometimes it improves an existing process or system. Sometimes the right recommendation is to address a more fundamental business or technology issue first.

We start with the problem, not the tool.

Our approach

How we work

We begin by learning how the business actually operates. What takes too long? Where does information get lost? What frustrates customers or employees? Which processes depend too heavily on one person? Where could better data, automation, or decision support make a meaningful difference?

From there, we help separate interesting possibilities from useful ones. The right first project should be valuable enough to matter, focused enough to complete, and practical enough for the organization to adopt. It should create confidence and capability, not dependency.

Clients work directly with our team throughout the process. We lead every engagement together, and depending on the work, you may work with Greg, an AI strategist, or a forward deployed engineer. The common thread is that the people who understand your problem are the people building the solution, from diagnosis through delivery.

How we operate

Principles behind the work

These are the operating beliefs that guide every engagement and every recommendation we make.

Start with the problem

Technology choices come after we understand the business outcome. The tool follows the need, not the other way around.

Make value visible

A project should have a clear reason to exist and a practical way to determine whether it is working. No vague success criteria.

Design for the people using it

The best system is not the one with the most features. It is the one people can understand, trust, and use effectively.

Use AI deliberately

AI is powerful, but it is not automatically the right solution. We use it when its capabilities genuinely match the problem.

Build capability, not dependency

Clients should understand what has been built and retain control over their tools, processes, data, and knowledge.

Build for change

A useful system should solve today's problem without creating a dead end. We design with flexibility to adapt as business and technology evolve.
