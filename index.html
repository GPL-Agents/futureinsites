Selected Case Work

Three case studies. Three angles on AI value.

An NLP sentiment study of a major automotive brand. Investment-grade diligence on an AI infrastructure business. Forecasting analysis of AI's impact on an incumbent platform's economics. Three angles on how AI is changing how value is created, captured, and measured.

TESLA
Automotive · NLP & Sentiment Analysis

An NLP case study on Tesla's Twitter brand across 2018 and 2020. ~2.4M tweets analyzed through VADER, a default BERT classifier, and a BERT model fine-tuned on Sentiment140. Findings on engagement-weighted sentiment, hashtag evolution, and what model choice reveals about domain match.

VADER & BERT Engagement-Weighted Sentiment Social Listening
ORACLE
AI Infrastructure · Investment Diligence

An Investment Committee memo analyzing Oracle's $455B AI backlog. Two-scenario DCF, GPU economic obsolescence vs. extended GAAP useful lives, customer concentration across NVIDIA, AMD, and OpenAI. Strong Underweight; new normal fair value $75 vs. $164.52.

Two-Scenario DCF GPU Obsolescence Customer Concentration
Google
Incumbent Platform · AI Economics

A two-engine business architecture modeling Search/Services and Cloud separately. AI Overviews monetization, Gemini long-query monetization, and the 2026 CapEx / D&A trough explicitly preserved. Net call: AI raises continuing value, modestly. Maintain.
