What we build into the site

Get found. Get booked. Get work done.

Get found in search

Search engine optimization, title tags, descriptions, structured data, internal linking, performance, accessibility, and local search foundations. We also shape content for AI search, including GEO and AEO.

Online orders and scheduling

Real availability, not a contact form: services, staff, buffers, deposits, reminders, calendar sync. The customer books in 30 seconds; you see it on your phone.

Collect the right information

Forms that create the right record in the right system and flag what is missing before it becomes a follow-up.

Manage client work anywhere

Files, approvals, e-signatures, status, schedules, quotes, and follow-ups in one place, designed to work from the owner's phone.
