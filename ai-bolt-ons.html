AI Bolt-Ons

A reference index of the plugins, skills, MCP connectors, and browser agents you can install on top of your AI platform. Built for operators getting beyond chat with Claude, ChatGPT, and similar.

How to read this page

The AI Vendor Landscape covers the platforms themselves. This page covers what to add on top: plugins (bundles of skills and tools), skills (single-purpose know-how), and MCP servers (connectors to your apps and data). Different shopping moment, different mental model. Pick a category, scan the cards, and do your own due diligence before installing anything.

Plugins

Bundles of capability you install in one motion: a plugin can ship dozens of skills, prompts, and tools tuned for a domain. The highest-leverage install if you want a whole specialist team showing up at once.

03

MCP Servers

Connectors that let your AI read and act on the rest of your stack. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard that finally makes "your AI can use my tools" a normal sentence instead of a custom build. Three flavors below: direct hooks into apps you already use, specialized tools that bring their own AI capability, and integration platforms that bundle thousands of services behind one endpoint.

granola
granola.ai
MCP

Feeds every meeting note to Claude. Granola already records and summarizes your calls; the MCP lets Claude pull those notes into any later workflow without manual copy-paste.

Claude
slack
modelcontextprotocol.io
MCP

Posts updates and reads channel history on your behalf. Workflows finally get real workplace context instead of you pasting threads in. The single most useful MCP for most teams.

Claude
notion
notion.so
MCP

Reads and writes your databases and docs. Claude can query Notion as a knowledge base, then update pages from a conversation. Best paired with operators who already live in Notion.

Claude
kondo
claude.ai/plugins
MCP

Flags which LinkedIn DMs actually need a reply. The first useful AI take on relationship inbox triage: skips the noise, surfaces the conversations that move work forward.

MCP

Token-efficient browser automation. A sandboxed browser the AI controls so it can fill forms and pull info from pages without a public API, without burning your context window on raw HTML.
