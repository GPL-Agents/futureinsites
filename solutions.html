Solutions

How we work with you

FutureInSites delivers AI strategy, transformation, and tooling across three audiences: individuals who want a personal AI agent of their own, businesses scaling from SMB through enterprise, and investors building a thesis around the AI ecosystem.

Professionals

AI for Operators & Executives

Done-for-you personal AI agent setups for professionals, executives, and high-output operators who want an always-on assistant they actually own. Configured to your workflow, your tools, and your data, with no third-party SaaS dependency for the agent itself.

Personal AI Agent Setup

A fully configured AI agent running on dedicated hardware behind your own VPN. Custom personality tuned to your workflow, with native integration to Telegram, Gmail, Google Calendar, and your existing tools. Includes hardware sourcing guidance, secure remote access via Tailscale, agent personality tuning, and a workflow training session so you can extend the agent yourself. Built on OpenClaw, an open architecture designed for personal AI assistants.

Engagement from $2,500
Business

AI strategy and transformation for SMB through enterprise

From readiness assessment to roadmap to live build, we work alongside your team to translate AI capability into real business outcomes. Engagements follow a Build–Operate–Transfer model: we set the foundation, build with your team, then transfer ownership so your organization keeps moving after we leave.

AI Readiness Assessment

A structured audit of your data infrastructure, talent, culture, and existing AI investments. Outputs include a capability heatmap, gap analysis, and a clear-eyed view of where you can move now versus what needs to be built first.

AI Strategy & Roadmap

A prioritized, phased AI strategy tied directly to your business objectives. Includes use case prioritization across a value vs. complexity framework, build/buy/partner decisions, governance frameworks, and investment sizing.

Vendor Selection & Architecture

Navigate the AI vendor landscape across model providers, infrastructure, and applied AI software. We help you select, configure, and integrate the right platforms, with reference architectures that protect optionality as the landscape keeps shifting.

AI Governance & Ethics

Responsible AI frameworks, stage-gate investment criteria, and Centers of Excellence that scale your AI program without unmanaged risk. Policy guardrails and model risk management that keep Board oversight honest as deployments multiply.

AI Build & Integration

Build and integrate AI capability across your business. Custom agent development for specific workflows, multi-step workflow orchestration across internal and external systems, and third-party AI platform integration (OpenAI, Anthropic, vertical SaaS).

Embedded AI Engineering

Ongoing engineering support, upgrades, and maintenance for your AI systems. A Forward Deployed Engineer working a set number of hours each week to keep your stack current as the underlying tools, integrations, and bolt-ons change. Built for organizations that need ongoing engineering capacity without standing up an internal AI team.

Investor

Strategic counsel and market intelligence for investors

For fund partners, family offices, and corporate strategy teams making AI bets in a market that is reshaping itself faster than any annual report can track. We bring proprietary research, a working knowledge of the AI vendor universe, and the financial frameworks needed to size opportunity, risk, and timing.

Strategic Advisory

Strategic counsel for investors and the operators in their portfolios. Quarterly reviews, scenario planning, and a thinking partner for the calls that don't fit on a slide. DCF modeling, GPU economic depreciation, and forecast frameworks for M&A and capital decisions.

AI Market Intelligence

Bespoke research drawing on the AI Tech Landscape and our proprietary vendor coverage. Vendor due diligence for portfolio decisions, competitive landscape analyses for portfolio companies, thesis development for new sectors, and quarterly briefings on category shifts. Designed for VCs, PE, family offices, and corporate development teams.

Embedded AI Research

Custom AI tools tailored to your fund or strategy, paired with ongoing market research and category coverage on a recurring cadence. We build the system, run the research, and update both as the AI landscape shifts month by month.
