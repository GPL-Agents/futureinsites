Solutions

How we work with you

FutureInSites works with small and medium businesses, professional offices, and individuals who want AI working for them without needing a technical team to make it happen.

Small & Medium Businesses

AI strategy and implementation for field-first businesses

For construction companies, MEP contractors, industrial services firms, field trades, and suppliers: businesses where worker time is the most valuable asset and a dedicated IT team is not in the budget. We bring enterprise methodology at SMB scale, with engagements that follow a Build, Customize, Train model: we build the foundation, tailor it to your operations, and train your team to own it.

AI Readiness Assessment

A structured audit of your data infrastructure, talent, culture, and existing AI investments. Outputs include a capability heatmap, gap analysis, and a clear-eyed view of where you can move now versus what needs to be built first.

AI Strategy & Roadmap

A prioritized, phased AI strategy tied directly to your business objectives. Includes use case prioritization across a value vs. complexity framework, build/buy/partner decisions, governance frameworks, and investment sizing.

Vendor Selection & Architecture

Navigate the AI vendor landscape across model providers, infrastructure, and applied AI software. We help you select, configure, and integrate the right platforms, with reference architectures that protect optionality as the landscape keeps shifting.

AI Governance & Ethics

Responsible AI frameworks, stage-gate investment criteria, and Centers of Excellence that scale your AI program without unmanaged risk. Policy guardrails and model risk management that keep Board oversight honest as deployments multiply.

AI Build & Integration

Build and integrate AI capability across your business. Custom agent development for specific workflows, multi-step workflow orchestration across internal and external systems, and third-party AI platform integration (OpenAI, Anthropic, vertical SaaS).

Embedded AI Engineering

Ongoing engineering support, upgrades, and maintenance for your AI systems. A Forward Deployed Engineer working a set number of hours each week to keep your stack current as the underlying tools, integrations, and bolt-ons change. Built for organizations that need ongoing engineering capacity without standing up an internal AI team.

Proof-of-concept implementation typically ranges from $10K to $30K.Build, implementation, training, user guides, and one year of support are included.

Professional Offices

AI that reduces admin and reclaims billable time

For dental practices, law firms, chiropractic offices, accounting firms, and similar professional practices: businesses where the most valuable hours are spent with patients and clients, not on scheduling, documentation, intake, and follow-up. We tailor AI to your practice management workflows so your team spends less time on back-office drag and more time on what they were trained to do.

Workflow Automation

AI-powered automation for intake, scheduling, documentation, follow-up, and billing workflows. Built around your existing practice management software, not instead of it.

AI Build & Integration

Custom agent development and integration for your practice. Front-desk agents, documentation assistants, referral coordination, and client communication tools built to your specific workflows.

Embedded AI Engineering

Ongoing support for practices that want to keep improving after initial setup: new integrations, staff training on new capabilities, and upgrades as the tools evolve.

Individuals

A personal AI built for the life you actually run

For individuals managing multiple business interests, investment portfolios, family obligations, philanthropic commitments, and international travel, the complexity of your life doesn't fit neatly into any one app. FutureInSites builds a custom AI infrastructure that works across all of it: scheduling across time zones, briefing you before meetings, tracking commitments across your business and personal worlds, and handling the administrative layer that consumes time you cannot afford to lose. Built on hardware you own, with no dependency on a third-party platform. Think of it as a chief of staff that never sleeps and never forgets.

Personal AI Agent Setup

A fully configured personal AI agent you can reach anytime via Telegram. No app to learn, no UI to manage — just message it like you would a person. Tuned to your workflow and connected to Gmail, Google Calendar, and your existing tools. Includes agent personality tuning, integration setup, and a training session so you can get the most out of it from day one. Built on OpenClaw, an open architecture designed for personal AI assistants.
