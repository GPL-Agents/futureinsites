Privacy Policy

What we collect on this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Who we are

This website, futureinsites.com, is operated by FutureInSites LLC ("FutureInSites," "we," "us"). This policy describes what information we collect when you visit the site and how we use it.

Information we collect

Usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, time on page, approximate geographic region, device and browser type, and how you arrived here. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally to us.

Information you provide. If you submit our contact form or email us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, company, and message. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and conduct business with you.

Cookies

The only cookies this site sets are those used by Google Analytics, and only if you accept them in the cookie banner. If you decline, Google Analytics never loads and no analytics cookies are set. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell or share your information with advertisers.

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Decline in the cookie banner when you first visit; your choice is remembered in your browser
  • Change your mind later by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which makes the banner ask again
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on

The site works fully with cookies declined or disabled.

How Google uses this data

Google Analytics is a service of Google LLC. Information about how Google processes data collected through sites that use its services is available at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings and then deleted automatically. Contact form submissions and email correspondence are kept as long as needed to handle your inquiry and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Your choices and rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us at privacy@futureinsites.com and we will respond promptly. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Third-party services

This site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may process technical request data (such as IP addresses) to serve and secure the site. Links to external sites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
