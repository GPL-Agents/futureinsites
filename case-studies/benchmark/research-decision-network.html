Decision network

Five roles may sit with different people.

The resident, researcher, influencer, payer, and approver may be one person or several people. Leadership shifts with urgency, care needs, cognition, and family circumstances.

01

Resident

Lives with the decision. Preferences, dignity, autonomy, and daily experience must remain central.

02

Researcher

Finds communities, compares options, gathers pricing, and organizes information.

03

Influencer

Builds or reduces confidence through advice, lived experience, and trusted relationships.

04

Payer

Assesses affordability, available assets, ongoing costs, and financial sustainability.

05

Approver

Can authorize, support, delay, or veto the final decision depending on the situation.

Implication for Benchmark

The inquiry process should identify who is searching and who else participates without assuming the adult child always controls the decision.
