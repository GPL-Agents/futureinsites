Implementation

Operating model

Clear ownership and response standards turn the recommendation into a repeatable system.

Inquiry build

Internal staff or a consultant builds the inquiry paths and lead scoring, connects them to the site chat and CRM, then tests and optimizes them.

Class kit

One standard class of about an hour on AI and scam safety, plus the registration form and data collection every location uses.

Local event team

At each community, one person recruits a local sponsor, learns the course and leads the event, with 1 to 2 helpers to check people in, collect information and help attendees.

Owners and responsibilities

Marketing owns the inquiry build, scoring and CRM handoff, with internal staff or a consultant. Each community names an event lead and 1 to 2 helpers. Sales counselors follow up in score order.

Workflow and SLAs

Event registration and check-in feed the CRM. Leads route by score, role and care level, with follow-up priority and an SLA. Response times TBD with Benchmark.

Budget and resources

Staff or consultant time for the inquiry build, class materials, event staff time, sponsor support, resident transportation, creative, and media. Dollar amounts TBD with Benchmark.
