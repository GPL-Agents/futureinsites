Appendix

One connected planning process

The work moves from evidence to audience understanding, campaign design, AI-enabled inquiry, and measurable implementation.

01

Research

Review market evidence, Benchmark materials, search behavior, decision roles, and competitive experiences.

02

Map

Translate the evidence into distinct resident and adult-child journeys.

03

Design

Develop the education campaign, messages, channels, and creative system.

04

Enable

Connect the AI-guided inquiry paths and lead scoring to the CRM, so follow-up matches each buyer journey: older adult, adult child, or caregiver and referral source.

05

Measure

Define KPIs, pilot learning, operating ownership, and implementation requirements.
