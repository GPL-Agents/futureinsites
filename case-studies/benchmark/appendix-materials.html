Appendix

Project materials

The recommendation combines client-provided evidence, secondary research, competitive review, and course frameworks.

Benchmark-provided materials

  • Project brief and campaign deck
  • Brand guidance and community brochures
  • Resident, family, care, and memory-care materials
  • Advertising, inquiry, and pricing experiences
  • Lead-scoring workbook and answer library

External and course materials

  • Senior living market and decision research
  • Buyer journey mapping framework
  • Search and competitor experience review
  • Channel, social, and technology-use research
  • AI, privacy, measurement, and governance sources

Evidence principle

Commercial datasets and platform patterns are treated as directional. They are not combined into universal population estimates.
