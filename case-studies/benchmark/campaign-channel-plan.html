Campaign deliverable 03

Campaign and channel plan

A repeatable local launch system that turns trusted partnerships into registrations, attendance, lead capture, follow-up, and original content.

The local campaign sequence

01

Recruit partner

Select a community center, YMCA, library, faith group, club, or municipal program.

02

Build local demand

Launch co-branded search, calendar, media, email, social, and community outreach.

03

Register and remind

Capture advance registrations while welcoming walk-ins through a simple event check-in.

04

Deliver and survey

Teach useful skills, collect feedback, and obtain consent for relevant follow-up.

05

Follow up and publish

Route leads, offer next sessions, create local content, and aggregate research findings.
