Campaign deliverable 03

Campaign and channel plan

A repeatable local launch system that turns trusted partnerships into registrations, attendance, lead capture, follow-up, and original content.

A second acquisition path: financial planning

Search demand

Create useful pages around senior living cost, affordability, local pricing, cost comparison, and financial planning questions.

Interactive conversion

Use a multi-step calculator to turn current expenses and local Benchmark price ranges into a meaningful result.

First-party lead capture

Offer saved results, Financial Concierge help, community exploration, or a tour so Benchmark can begin the relationship directly.

View the cost comparison strategy.

The local campaign sequence

01

Recruit partner

Select a community center, YMCA, library, faith group, club, or municipal program.

02

Build local demand

Launch co-branded search, calendar, media, email, social, and community outreach.

03

Register and remind

Capture advance registrations while welcoming walk-ins through a simple event check-in.

04

Deliver and survey

Teach useful skills, collect feedback, and obtain consent for relevant follow-up.

05

Follow up and publish

Route leads, offer next sessions, create local content, and aggregate research findings.

Campaign framework calendar

Sixteen nested programs across the four program families, sequenced over the first twelve months of the campaign. Always-on programs (stories, social, search, nurture, ambassadors, lead intake) start in months 1 to 3 and run through month 12; quarterly beats (events, leadership pieces, technology sessions) land in months 3, 6, 9 and 12; pilots run early so results inform the rest of the year.
