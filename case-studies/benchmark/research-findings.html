Research synthesis

Six findings shape the campaign.

The strategy follows the evidence from early practicality through trust, experience, and selection.

01

Resident leadership is strongest in planned moves

Independent living searches more often begin with the future resident.

02

Practical fit has a clear sequence

Location comes first, then price, availability, and care fit.

03

People are the proof

Peers, residents, families, and trusted local organizations shape confidence.

04

The tour converts claims into evidence

Prospects evaluate people, place, food, activities, apartments, and everyday life.

05

Local intent dominates search

Local pages and financial-planning content can capture high-intent demand.

06

Facebook reaches both primary personas

Audience-specific social creative can reach older adults and adult children separately.
