Recommended next steps

Implementation plan

A phased path from one local campaign pilot and a limited inquiry prototype to a repeatable program supported by CRM data, operating ownership, and measured learning.

Phased roadmap

01

Define

Confirm pilot goals, communities, audience, partner criteria, data fields, and success measures.

02

Build

Complete curriculum, creative, landing page, forms, CRM mapping, and staff workflows.

03

Pilot

Launch in selected communities, observe delivery, measure participation, and capture feedback.

04

Learn

Review conversion, survey quality, partner performance, workload, and model calibration.

05

Scale

Refine the toolkit, approve governance, train teams, and expand to additional markets.

Operating model

Inquiry build

Internal staff or a consultant builds the inquiry paths and lead scoring, connects them to the site chat and CRM, then tests and optimizes them.

Class kit

One standard class of about an hour on AI and scam safety, plus the registration form and data collection every location uses.

Local event team

At each community, one person recruits a local sponsor, learns the course and leads the event, with 1 to 2 helpers to check people in, collect information and help attendees.

Owners and responsibilities

Marketing owns the inquiry build, scoring and CRM handoff, with internal staff or a consultant. Each community names an event lead and 1 to 2 helpers. Sales counselors follow up in score order.

Workflow and SLAs

Event registration and check-in feed the CRM. Leads route by score, role and care level, with follow-up priority and an SLA. Response times TBD with Benchmark.

Budget and resources

Staff or consultant time for the inquiry build, class materials, event staff time, sponsor support, resident transportation, creative, and media. Dollar amounts TBD with Benchmark.

Technical and governance requirements

CRM and data

Source, campaign, partner, community, event, journey role, survey, and outcome fields. Score, role and care level pass to the CRM with follow-up priority and SLA.

Model calibration

Calibrate answer points and weights on Benchmark's won and lost leads, then recheck as new outcomes arrive.

Fair housing

Care needs set care level only, not lead priority. Event venues must not signal a preference. Review ads and targeting.

Change management

Training, scripts, process documentation, feedback channels, adoption measures, and accountable leadership.
