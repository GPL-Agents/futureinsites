Recommended next steps

Implementation plan

A phased path from one local campaign pilot and a limited inquiry prototype to a repeatable program supported by CRM data, operating ownership, and measured learning.

Phased roadmap

01

Define

Confirm pilot goals, communities, audience, partner criteria, data fields, and success measures.

02

Build

Complete curriculum, creative, landing page, forms, CRM mapping, and staff workflows.

03

Pilot

Launch in selected communities, observe delivery, measure participation, and capture feedback.

04

Learn

Review conversion, survey quality, partner performance, workload, and model calibration.

05

Scale

Refine the toolkit, approve governance, train teams, and expand to additional markets.
