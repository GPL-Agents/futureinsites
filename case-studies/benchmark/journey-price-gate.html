Gate 02

Residents and adult children need different financial evidence.

After location establishes viability, a transparent financial checkpoint helps the decision network determine whether the journey can continue.

Resident requirement

Confidence and control

Compare the full cost of the current lifestyle with senior living, understand what is included, and preserve agency over the decision.

Adult child requirement

Clarity and coordination

Organize expenses, assess support costs, share assumptions, and identify a realistic next step with the parent.

Experience requirement

Transparent results

Use real inputs, local price ranges, visible assumptions, and editable results.

Connected recommendation

Open the proposed Cost Comparison experience.
