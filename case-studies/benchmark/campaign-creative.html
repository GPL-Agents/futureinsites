Creative inventory

Six sets, built across three audiences

Each set carries the versions its channel needs, so one campaign idea can meet the person who will attend, the person who will book it, and the person who has already been targeted. Dates, times, and registration links are placeholders until the Hanover event is confirmed.

One campaign system, not twelve mockups

The pieces are built as coordinated families: a paid ad points at the landing page, the landing page points at the class, the class hands off the take-home material, and the take-home material points at the next session. Series C adds the scam-aware entry point without breaking that path, and each asset names its audience, channel, objective, buying stage, and call to action so the handoffs can be checked rather than assumed.
