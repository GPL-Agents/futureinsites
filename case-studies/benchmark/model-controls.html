How to edit and use the model
Score a leadUse Lead Scorer to answer inquiry questions. Unknown values remain neutral. Predictions, coverage, and margins update immediately.
Change weightsUse values from 0 to 10. Zero removes that variable from a prediction. Each prediction normalizes its own weights.
Interpret certaintyCoverage measures how much weighted evidence is known. Margin measures the gap between the top two classes.
Use care variables carefullyDaily living, memory, and medical needs affect care-level fit only. They do not raise or lower conversion priority.

The scoring model makes its assumptions transparent. Benchmark can calibrate answer points and weights against closed-won and lost leads as part of ongoing CRM model governance.
