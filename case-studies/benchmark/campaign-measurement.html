Volume-based gap

Benchmark's INQ to MQL rate (6.2%) is well below best practice (8.5%).

ABM-focused gaps

Benchmark's MQL to SAL (51.0%) and SAL to SQL (41.0%) are well below best practice (69.8% and 54.7%).

Key takeaway

At Benchmark's current rates, the ABM-focused funnel wins 0.88% of inquiries vs. 0.61% volume-based, about 44% more. At best-practice rates, ABM needs 66 inquiries per win vs. 112 volume-based.

Source: Benchmark Waterfall Data, Fall 2026 (team campaign deck draft, Nov 17, 2026). Percentage won is the product of the four stage rates; top of funnel needed is 1 divided by percentage won.
