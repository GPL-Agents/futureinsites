Campaign deliverable 06

Measurement plan

A shared scorecard connects local reach and class participation to lead creation, tours, move-ins, marketing content, and original case study data.

One measurement chain from awareness to move-in

01

Reach

Local impressions, partner distribution, search visibility, page visits, and earned coverage.

02

Participation

Registrations, walk-ins, attendance rate, repeat interest, and survey completion.

03

Lead creation

Consent, CRM records, inquiry completion, advisor conversations, and qualified interest.

04

Conversion

Tours, deposits, move-ins, conversion rates, velocity, and event-sourced revenue.

05

Authority

Original findings, content outputs, media use, backlinks, executive visibility, and citations.

Reverse waterfall

Targets working backward from move-ins to tours, qualified leads, attendees, registrations, and reach.

Presentation space

Attribution design

Presentation space

Presentation space

Reporting cadence

Presentation space
