Campaign deliverable 02

Positioning and messaging

One campaign promise, adapted to the distinct motivations of older adults, adult children, local partners, and professional referral sources.

Core position

Confidence grows through practical education and trusted support

Older adults

Lead with independence, useful tools, control, confidence, and the ability to recognize risks without creating fear.

Adult children

Lead with reassurance that a parent will receive patient guidance, current safety education, and ongoing human support.

Community partners

Lead with local service, accessible education, shared promotion, and useful programming for their existing members.

Referral sources

Lead with evidence that Benchmark supports safer technology use, family communication, and informed decision making.

Presentation space

Message hierarchy

Final core promise, three supporting proof points, calls to action, and approved campaign language.

Presentation space

Message variations

Final headlines, body copy, objections, proof, and calls to action by audience and buying stage.

Messaging principle

The campaign should make AI feel useful and manageable. Safety is a reason to pay attention, while confidence, connection, and independence are the reasons to participate.
