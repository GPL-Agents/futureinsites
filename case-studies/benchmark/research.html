Senior living is chosen by a network, not one buyer.

Pressure builds across four areas

HEALTH AND FUNCTION

A change raises concern

Falls, medication difficulty, mobility loss, cognitive change, or uncertainty about future care can make the current arrangement feel less sustainable.12

HOME AND ENVIRONMENT

The house becomes harder to manage

Maintenance, stairs, transportation, neighborhood safety, or the loss of a spouse can shift the balance between familiarity and burden.12

FAMILY AND CAREGIVING

The support system reaches a limit

Caregiver strain, distance, family disagreement, or an urgent hospital or rehabilitation discharge can accelerate the search.2

SOCIAL AND PSYCHOLOGICAL

Connection competes with attachment

Loneliness and a desire for community coexist with fear of losing independence, attachment to home, and anxiety about relocation.12

The resident, researcher, influencer, payer, and final approver may be the same person or several people. Leadership shifts with care level, cognition, urgency, and family circumstances.

Six findings shape the campaign

01

Resident leadership is strongest in planned moves

About 80% of users of an independent living oriented downsizing tool were older adults, while about 90% of users of a memory care tool were family or others. These are platform patterns, not population estimates.22

02

Practical fit is the first gate

Price, location, availability, and care level determine which communities remain viable. Experience differentiates the options that pass.34

03

People are the proof

Among adults ages 60 to 90 who would consider senior living, 54% said knowing a resident shaped their views, and 43% said private access to residents or families would increase trust.8

04

The tour converts claims into evidence

Prospects observe staff behavior, meals, activities, apartments, and everyday community life. One qualitative study found families narrowed the field to about three communities and toured each.37

05

Local intent dominates search

Ten of the top 15 terms in the team keyword sample included “near me.” Cost queries were lower volume but cheaper and likely closer to active consideration.

06

Facebook reaches both primary personas

Facebook is used daily by 45% of adults 65+ and 54% of adults ages 50 to 64. YouTube supports searchable tour and resident video.35

Evidence note. Studies cited here measure different populations and stages. Commercial datasets from Roobrik, A Place for Mom, Caring.com, and other providers are directional. Their percentages should not be combined into a single funnel or treated as universal population estimates.
