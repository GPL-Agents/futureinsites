AI application and integration

AI in the marketing toolkit

Benchmark's AI recommendations work in two places: the Connected with Confidence classes, which market about AI, and an AI-optimized inquiry funnel, which markets with AI. Inside the funnel, AI applies at two points that sync together, and each adds value on its own.

Benchmark's AI recommendations

MARKETING ABOUT AI

AI outreach classes

Connected with Confidence: free local classes, hosted with a community partner, on using AI and technology with confidence and avoiding scams and fraud. Attendees become registrations, survey responses and tours. See the campaign.

MARKETING WITH AI

AI-optimized inquiry funnel

AI applied inside the inquiry process at two points: scoring each lead, and customizing the inquiry path each visitor follows. Both are explained below.

Two AI applications in the funnel

1. AI lead scoring

Scores each inquirer in real time from 16 signals in the inquiry form, calls, web behavior and the CRM. Five predictions, each with a certainty: who is searching, who is driving, who decides, care level needed, and likelihood to convert.

On its own: ranks the inquiries already arriving through today's forms, calls and CRM, so counselors reach Hot, high-certainty leads first.

Try the lead scorer, see the model controls, or see how it keeps learning.

2. AI-guided inquiry paths

The same model runs the inquiry experience: separate paths for adult children and older adults, branching like a decision tree, so each visitor gets the next question, content and step that fits. Today's site chat offers one path to everyone.

On its own: each visitor gets language, proof and next steps that fit who they are, starting with the first question.

See the inquiry design.

How they sync

The guided path collects the signals the score needs, and the score shapes the next step in the path. Score, role and care level pass to the CRM with follow-up priority and SLA. Role sets which buyer journey's follow-up each lead gets: older adult, adult child, or caregiver and referral source. Hot leads (score 70+) with high certainty get first follow-up. Event registrations from Connected with Confidence and answers from the cost comparison feed the same score.

Effort and expected impact

Effort: internal staff or a consultant builds the inquiry paths and scoring, connects them to the site chat and CRM, then tests and optimizes them. Impact: every inquirer gets a path that fits, and counselors reach Hot, high-certainty leads first.

Operating requirements

Chat vendor capability, CRM integration, and calibration of the model on Benchmark's won and lost leads. After launch, ongoing optimization: a scheduled AI process tunes the model weekly or monthly on new CRM outcomes, and each community gets its own tuned model that matches the care it offers, its local pricing and its distance buckets. See model tuning.

Governance guardrails

Care needs set care level only, not lead priority (fair housing). Unknown answers count as neutral, never as bad. Model changes are reviewed and approved before they go live.
