Marketing with AI

AI recommendations

One AI lead scoring model, built into a customized inquiry process, so every inquirer gets the right experience and the right follow-up.

Two connected recommendations

1. AI lead scoring

Scores each inquirer in real time from 16 signals in the inquiry form, calls, web behavior and the CRM. Five predictions, each with a certainty: who is searching, who is driving, who decides, care level needed, and likelihood to convert. Try the lead scorer.

2. AI-guided inquiry paths

The same model runs the inquiry experience: separate paths for adult children and older adults, branching like a decision tree, so each visitor gets the next question, content and step that fits. Today's site chat offers one path to everyone. See the inquiry design.

How they connect

Score, role and care level pass to the CRM with follow-up priority and SLA. Role sets which buyer journey's follow-up each lead gets: older adult, adult child, or caregiver and referral source. Hot leads (score 70+) with high certainty get first follow-up. Event registrations from Connected with Confidence feed the model and attribution.

Effort and expected impact

Effort: internal staff or a consultant builds the inquiry paths and scoring, connects them to the site chat and CRM, then tests and optimizes them. Impact: every inquirer gets a path that fits, and counselors reach Hot, high-certainty leads first.

Operating requirements

Chat vendor capability, CRM integration, and calibration of the model on Benchmark's won and lost leads.

Governance guardrails

Care needs set care level only, not lead priority (fair housing). Unknown answers count as neutral, never as bad.
