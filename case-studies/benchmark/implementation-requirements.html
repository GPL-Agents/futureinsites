Implementation

Technical and governance requirements

The program must be measurable, privacy-aware, operationally realistic, and consistent with fair housing obligations.

CRM and data

Capture source, campaign, partner, community, event, journey role, survey, and outcome fields. Score, role and care level pass to the CRM with follow-up priority and SLA.

Model calibration

Calibrate answer points and weights on Benchmark's won and lost leads, then recheck as new outcomes arrive.

Fair housing

Care needs set care level only, not lead priority. Event venues must not signal a preference. Review ads and targeting.

Change management

Provide training, scripts, process documentation, feedback channels, adoption measures, and accountable leadership.
