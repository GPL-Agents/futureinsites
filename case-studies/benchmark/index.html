Campaign strategy, buyer journeys and AI lead scoring

A clearer path for adult child decision makers.

Helping families move forward with confidence, the strategy increases direct focus on adult children through a custom buyer journey, a unique inquiry process, and ad creative designed around their questions, concerns, and role in the decision.

The assignment

A connected campaign recommendation

Benchmark asked the team to develop an AI-centered marketing campaign for older adults and their families, paired with practical ways its marketing team could use AI to reach and convert prospects.

Academic project, not a FutureInSites engagement.
This website presents the completed Boston College campaign recommendation. FutureInSites provides hosting only.

Two workstreams reinforce one another

Recommendation 01, Marketing about AI

Connected with Confidence

In-person AI and technology safety training helps older adults and families use digital tools with confidence, recognize scams, and experience Benchmark as a trusted local resource.

Recommendation 02, Marketing with AI

More relevant inquiry and follow-up

Audience insight, conversational inquiry paths, live lead scoring, personalized content, and better measurement from inquiry through tour and move-in.

  • Distinct resident and adult child paths
  • Progressive questions that protect completion
  • Scores that update as new signals arrive
  • One CRM-ready profile across channels
