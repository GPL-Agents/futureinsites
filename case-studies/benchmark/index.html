Campaign strategy, buyer journeys and AI lead scoring

Helping families move forward, with a clearer path for adult child decision makers.

The strategy increases direct focus on adult children through a custom buyer journey, a unique inquiry process, and ad creative designed around their questions, concerns, and role in the decision.

The assignment

A connected campaign recommendation

Benchmark asked the team to develop an AI-centered marketing campaign for older adults and their families, paired with practical ways its marketing team could use AI to reach and convert prospects.

Academic project, not a FutureInSites engagement.
This website presents the completed Boston College campaign recommendation. FutureInSites provides hosting only.

Two workstreams reinforce one another

Marketing about AI

Connected with Confidence

A local education program that helps older adults and families use technology with confidence, recognize scams, and discover practical tools through trusted community events.

Marketing with AI

More relevant inquiry and follow-up

Audience insight, conversational inquiry paths, live lead scoring, personalized content, and better measurement from inquiry through tour and move-in.
